Public Infrastructure & Road Repair Guidance Overview

Step-by-step guidance for reporting damaged locality roads, open potholes, broken streetlights, and civic hazards to municipal corporations and CPGRAMS.

Step-by-Step Escalation Procedure

  1. Photograph the damaged road or civic hazard with geo-tagging if possible.
  2. Identify the responsible local authority (Municipal Corporation / PWD).
  3. Draft a formal complaint specifying location landmarks and safety risks.
  4. Lodge request on CPGRAMS under Ministry of Road Transport & Highways or local State Grievance portal.

Required Documents Checklist

  • Clear photos of road damage / pothole
  • Exact street address and landmark
  • Previous correspondence or ward complaint number (if any)
