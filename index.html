Frequently Asked Questions About NyayaSetu

How does NyayaSetu's AI multimodal intake work?

You can speak in 22 Indian languages (Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, etc.), type plain text, or upload photos of damaged infrastructure. Sarvam STT transcribes your voice, our LLM extracts structured facts, and resolves your exact administrative ward and nodal officer.

Does NyayaSetu require mandatory Aadhaar?

No. NyayaSetu strictly complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. We only use mobile phone OTP verification for tracking notifications. No Aadhaar or banking credentials are ever requested.

How are SLA deadlines and automatic escalations handled?

Each grievance is assigned a strict citizen charter SLA window (e.g. 24h for urgent water/sewer contamination, 48h for roads, 7 days for municipal billing). If the assigned junior engineer does not resolve the ticket in time, NyayaSetu automatically triggers Level 2 escalation to the Zonal Assistant Municipal Commissioner.

Is NyayaSetu an official government body?

NyayaSetu is an independent civic technology platform built to bridge the gap between citizens and government departments. It prepares formal legal drafts, formats evidence, and dispatches grievances directly to verified government endpoints (CPGRAMS, state PGR portals, and municipal APIs).

Are there any fees for citizens?

No. NyayaSetu is 100% free for all Indian citizens as a public service initiative.
