Clarity before complexity

NyayaSetu exists because the problem is usually not the absence of a government service — it's not knowing the right next step.

What NyayaSetu does

Helps you describe a problem, understand the relevant authority, prepare the right documents, draft your complaint, open the verified official portal, and track your case locally.

What NyayaSetu does NOT do

It is not a government website, does not submit complaints for you, does not provide legal advice, and never guarantees an outcome. Government portals are external destinations you act on yourself.

Privacy by design

We never ask for Aadhaar, PAN, OTPs, passwords, bank account or card numbers. Your tracker lives only in your browser's LocalStorage and never leaves your device.

Deterministic routing

Every route is a curated, human-verified rule. No AI/LLM ever invents a government authority or URL. Official sources carry a verified-on date.
